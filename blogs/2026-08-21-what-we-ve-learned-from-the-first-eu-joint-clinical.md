Search the first three EU Joint Clinical Assessment (JCA) reports for real-world evidence, and you won't find much. All three lean almost entirely on information drawn from randomized controlled trials or even single-arm clinical trials. That's a striking data point for anyone who has spent the past two years hearing that real-world evidence would be central to how Europe assesses new oncology medicines.
